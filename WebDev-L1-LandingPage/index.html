02 — What it does

Three habits, built in

◆

Session timing

Set a focus block, and Flowly holds the boundary — soft chimes at the start and finish, nothing in between.

◆

Notification hush

Every app goes quiet the moment a session starts. Urgent calls still get through — everything else waits.

◆

Weekly patterns

A simple log shows when your focus actually happens, so you can plan real work around your real rhythm.

03 — Why it exists

Attention is the scarce resource now

Most tools compete for your attention. Flowly is built to do the opposite — it steps out of the way once a session begins, and only speaks up when the work is done. No streaks to protect, no feed to check. Just a timer, a hush, and a record of the hours you actually gave to the thing that mattered.
